### Parking

We have a large parking lot on the five-acre property. Additional street parking is available along 26th St.

### What to bring

- Comfortable clothes you can move in
- A water bottle
- For aerial classes: tight-fitting pants or leggings that cover the backs of the knees
- A signed waiver before participating
